# ISO/IEC 42001 – AI Management System

> **Status:** draft for review · Reference: ISO/IEC 42001:2023. This is a gap overview for the ALITA Prototype, not a certification statement.

## Scope (clause 4)

- **AIMS scope:** development and internal operation of the ALITA Prototype, covering document chat, ingestion, summaries, clustering and the Graph RAG Graph Chat (typed input, read-aloud output).
- **Interested parties:**
  - researchers (users);
  - authors and cited persons (data subjects);
  - journals and co-authors (confidential manuscripts);
  - model providers (OpenRouter and the upstream LLM providers).
- **AI role:** AI provider and AI user (clause 4.1).

## Next steps

1. Name the system owner and adopt a one-page AI policy (A.2, A.3).
2. Complete the supplier assessment for OpenRouter and the upstream model providers, including where data is processed and whether it is retained (A.10).
3. Add server-side event logging of questions, retrieved sources, model and answer, with a defined retention period (A.6).
4. Define answer-quality monitoring, e.g. a monthly sample of 20 answers checked against their sources (clause 9).
